Q-Spot Hotspot — Household Network Policy

Welcome to Q-Spot Hotspot. By connecting to the Q-Spot Hotspot network and redeeming a voucher, you agree to follow these household rules. This network is set up to provide fast, fair, and reliable internet access for everyone in our home.

1. Who Can Use This Network

  • Family Members Only: Access to Q-Spot Hotspot is strictly limited to immediate family members living in or visiting the household.
  • No Sharing Outside the Home: The Wi-Fi signal is open, but internet access requires a voucher. Do not share voucher codes or QR access cards with neighbors, friends, or people outside the house without permission from the network administrator.

2. Vouchers & Pisonet-Style Access

  • Voucher System: Internet access is managed using time-based vouchers (similar to a Pisonet setup). Once a voucher’s time runs out, your connection will pause until a new voucher is entered.
  • One Voucher, One User: Each voucher is assigned to the first device that uses it. The same device may sign in again until the voucher expires, but a voucher cannot be shared with or transferred to a second device.
  • Fair Use & Turn-Taking: Vouchers are for personal use only. Please do not hoard multiple vouchers or bypass the timer system so that everyone in the family gets a fair turn online.
  • No Tampering: Do not attempt to hack, bypass, pause, or manipulate the hotspot portal, router settings, or voucher timer.

3. Family-Friendly Acceptable Use

Because this is a shared family network, everyone is expected to keep their online activity safe, respectful, and legal:

  • Keep It Safe & Wholesome: Accessing adult content, explicit material, violent websites, or gambling platforms is strictly prohibited.
  • Respect Bandwidth: Heavy downloads (such as large games, torrents, or 4K video backups) should be scheduled during off-peak hours (such as late at night) so you do not slow down the internet for family members who are studying, working, or watching shows.
  • Stay Legal: Do not use the network for pirating copyrighted movies or software, spreading malware, or engaging in any illegal online activities.
  • Online Kindness: Do not use our home internet for cyberbullying, harassment, or trolling.

4. Privacy & Household Data

  • Kept Strictly Within the Family: Any basic connection logs (such as device names, voucher usage times, and data speed metrics) stay strictly within the household.
  • System Improvement Only: This information is only checked by the family network administrator to troubleshoot dead zones, manage internet speeds, and improve the Q-Spot Hotspot system for everyone. Your personal browsing data is never sold, shared, or shown to anyone outside the family.

5. Service Availability & Rule Violations

  • Best-Effort Connection: While we aim to keep Q-Spot Hotspot running smoothly 24/7, internet speeds and uptime depend on our main internet provider and power supply. Occasional restarts or maintenance may happen.
  • Consequences of Breaking the Rules: If a family member breaks these rules (such as visiting blocked sites, tampering with the voucher system, or sharing access outside the house), their device may be temporarily paused or blocked from using Q-Spot Hotspot.
